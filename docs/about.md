I started in experimental particle physics, in the CMS experiment at the LHC,
where I searched for rare decays of the Higgs boson. I have since moved to
machine-learning approaches to physics.

Current projects:

- **Binary neutron stars in LIGO data.** Small state space models that estimate
  source parameters directly from detector strain.
- **An agent for LIGO.**
- **Superconducting magnet control.**
