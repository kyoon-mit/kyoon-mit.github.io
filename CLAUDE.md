# kyoon-mit.github.io

Personal research site of Kyungseop Yoon (MIT). Jekyll, served by GitHub Pages
from `docs/` on `main`, at https://kyungseop-yoon.com (see `CNAME`;
kyoon-mit.github.io redirects there). Each research project has its own Claude
session; this file tells any of them how to add to the site.

## Who it is for and how it should read

- Visitors: collaborators, people from talks, hiring committees.
- Plain, first-person, factual. No taglines, no "at the intersection of", no
  marketing adjectives, no em dashes in new prose. The owner finds generic,
  AI-sounding copy embarrassing; when in doubt, write less.
- Facts only. Do not invent project descriptions, results, dates or venues. If
  something is unknown, leave the field empty or ask.
- Background, accurately: formerly CMS (rare Higgs decays); now machine-learning
  approaches to physics. Current projects: LIGO binary neutron star time series,
  an agent for LIGO, superconducting magnet control. Not collider work now.

## Look

White background, dark navy ink and accent (the owner's CERN slide palette),
IBM Plex Sans throughout, light mode only. Colors are the tokens at the top of
`docs/assets/css/main.css`; use them, never new literal colors. No warm/orange
palettes, no serif display faces, no decorative italics.

## Never publish

- Unpublished results of collaborators, or their names attached to them. Say
  "internal results" and give at most orders of magnitude.
- Anything from private meetings, referee reports, or internal review.
- Credentials, internal paths, cluster details.

## Layout

```
docs/
  _config.yml            site title, tagline, links, collections
  _data/projects.yml     project cards on the home page (current and past)
  _data/bns_live.yml     generated by tools/bns_live.py; do not hand-edit
  _layouts/              default, home, page, project, update
  _includes/             header, footer, project-subnav (BNS menu), figure, plot
  index.md, about.md     home and about
  projects/<name>.md     single-page projects (geant4, higgs-rare-decays, ...)
  projects/bns/          the BNS section: overview, story, background,
                         related-work, live, brainstorm, updates, report, results
  _updates/              dated research-log entries (currently BNS only)
  assets/css/main.css    all styles; colors are tokens at the top
  assets/img/<project>/  figures, one folder per project, dated subfolders
tools/
  bns_live.py            refreshes the BNS live-runs page from wandb
  refresh_live.sh        wrapper that commits and pushes only real changes
```

## Adding a new project

1. Add or fill its card in `docs/_data/projects.yml` (`title`, `blurb`,
   `detail`, `tags`, `image`, `url`). Leave `url` empty until a page exists.
2. A short project: one page, `docs/projects/<name>.md`, `layout: page`.
3. A project with a running log, like BNS: copy the BNS pattern.
   - Pages under `docs/projects/<name>/` with `layout: project`.
   - A side menu: copy `_includes/project-subnav.html` to
     `_includes/<name>-subnav.html`, and make `_layouts/project.html` and
     `_layouts/update.html` pick the menu by a `project` front-matter key.
   - The `updates` collection's permalink is hard-wired to
     `/projects/bns/updates/:name/`. To give another project its own log,
     change it to use the entry's `project` key, or add a second collection.
     Check that the BNS update URLs do not change, since pages link to them.

## Adding to the BNS section

- **Update entry:** `docs/_updates/YYYY-MM-DD-short-slug.md` with front
  matter `title`, `date`, `summary`. Add a `talk:` block (`title`, `venue`,
  `about`) when the entry was presented as a talk; it gets a Talk tag.
- **Figures:** go in `docs/assets/img/bns/YYYY-MM-DD/`. Use
  `{% include figure.html src=... alt=... caption=... %}`, inside
  `<div class="plot-pair">` for two side by side. Keep PNGs under about 300 KB.
- **Tables:** inside an HTML `<div>` need `markdown="1"` on the div, or
  kramdown will not render them.
- **Brainstorm:** `docs/projects/bns/brainstorm.md`. When a test is done, move
  its item to Done, tick it (`class="is-done"`), and link the dated update.
- **Story:** `docs/projects/bns/story.md` is the narrative; extend the
  chapter list when a new phase starts.
- **Live runs:** edit the `RUNS` table in `tools/bns_live.py`, then
  `uv run --script tools/bns_live.py` (needs wandb credentials on the machine).

## Building and checking

There is no Ruby on the training machine, and nothing should be installed
outside an isolated env (prefer uv). GitHub Pages builds on push; a local
build is optional. If one is needed, use a throwaway Ruby env in the session
scratchpad and keep `HOME` and gem paths inside it.

Before pushing, at least check for broken internal links and that new tables
and figures render.

## Publishing

- Commit with a plain-English subject line; push only when the owner asks.
- Pushing to `main` publishes the site within a minute or two.
